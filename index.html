New Doun Kok Village

Doun Kok Village Community Workshop

Step into Doun Kok and spend the day making alongside the families who live there. Learn the village's craft from the people who keep it alive, share a home-cooked meal, and leave with something you made yourself.

Duration
Half Day
Group size
Max 12 people
Language
EN, KH
Level
All ages welcome
  • Welcome — meet your village host and walk through Doun Kok
  • Hands-on session — learn and make with local makers
  • Shared meal — Khmer food cooked with host families
  • Take it home — keep what you made
Your booking goes straight to the Doun Kok host families and makers — every workshop directly supports the village.
Workshop from
$15 / person
