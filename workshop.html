VERANDA POTTERY · TONLE BASSAC · PHNOM PENH
Pottery Workshop

Veranda works with the villagers of Andong Russei in Kampong Chhnang, where families have made pottery for more than a thousand years, creating new designs that honour their ancestors' craft. In the pottery workshop you create something with your own hands, then rest in a warm, peaceful space with homemade bread and coffee. Every piece helps sustain the village.

Read Veranda's story

In Kampong Chhnang, Cambodia, lies Andong Russei, a village that has crafted pottery for more than a thousand years. For generations, the villagers made small traditional clay pots, shaping their lives around this ancient craft. But as modern life rushed in, their pottery sold less and less, and many had no choice but to leave behind their heritage and work in nearby factories.

Veranda was born from a wish to preserve this beautiful tradition. Together with the villagers, we create pottery with new designs while honoring the spirit of their ancestors. We are here to share the timeless culture and stories of Cambodia with the world.

On December 13, 2025, we opened our doors. Every piece of pottery you purchase becomes a part of protecting and sustaining this village.

We also prepare small homemade breads and coffee brewed with carefully selected beans. Here, you will find a warm and peaceful space to rest, and a pottery workshop where you can create something with your own hands.

Together, in love.
